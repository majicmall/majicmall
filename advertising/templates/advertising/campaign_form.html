How rotation works: Each position receives one display during every complete rotation loop. For example, a property with a 10-second display time and 10 positions has a 100-second loop. Purchasing two positions gives the advertiser two displays during every loop. Exclusive campaigns reserve the entire property.
Campaign budget rules: Standard inventory begins at $25. Up to two standard locations requires at least $50. Premium inventory such as the Homepage Billboard requires at least $50. Majestic Square Signature inventory may require $250 or more. The final minimum is the greater of the location-count minimum, property minimum, and combined lease-plan cost.
