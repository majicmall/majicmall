MajicMall Megaverse Storefront Standards

Storefront imagery must professionally represent your business, accurately reflect your products or services, and visually complement your intended MajicMall Megaverse Zone. Images must be polished, appropriate, safe, and free of misleading or unauthorized branding. MajicMall Megaverse administrators may request revisions or replace imagery that does not meet these standards.

  • • Recommended landscape image: 1600 × 900 pixels or larger
  • • Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, or WEBP
  • • Use a clear, professional, well-lit storefront presentation
  • • Match the visual character of your selected Zone
